# Roadmap

`main` is Home Scanner v1: indoor RoomPlan scans only.

The `v2-outdoor` branch adds the outdoor mesh scan, the aerial underlay, and version 2 of `roomscan.home`. What is still not built:

- Device pins on an outdoor zone or on the site photo. Pins stay on indoor rooms.
- A live Home Assistant client.
- Moving indoor pins when RoomPlan's multi-room alignment changes coordinates.
- More than one floor, and editing walls after a scan.
